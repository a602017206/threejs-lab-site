# Poke1 data sources

This directory is generated by `scripts/poke1/build-catalog.mjs`.

- [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co/) — species names, genera, flavor text, types, evolution-chain data, and official-artwork endpoint paths.
- [PokéAPI sprites repository](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites) — source PNGs converted locally to WebP. Its artwork is derivative Pokémon intellectual property; retain this attribution and review its licence/usage terms before redistribution.
- [Bulbapedia — List of Pokémon by animated series debut](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon_by_animated_series_debut) — animated-series episode references. Episode dates are intentionally `null` unless an authoritative per-episode date source is added.

Pokémon and Pokémon character names are trademarks of Nintendo, Game Freak, and Creatures. This fan catalog is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

The catalog includes the first 251 Pokémon, including Johto (#152–251). The displayed game models are obtained from [pokemon-party/3d-pokemon](https://github.com/pokemon-party/3d-pokemon); rights-holder authorization has not been confirmed. The sources for artwork and species information remain those listed above.
